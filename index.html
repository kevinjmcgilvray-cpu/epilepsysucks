Why CURE Epilepsy

Every mile funds a search for an actual cure

I’m running for CURE Epilepsy, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit research organization — donations made through my fundraiser are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

CURE Epilepsy funds patient-focused research aimed at curing epilepsy, not just managing it. Since its founding, the organization has put more than $100 million into epilepsy research and funded over 300 grants across 19 countries.

My own epilepsy has meant three surgeries, years of medication trial-and-error, and a brain that still misfires about once a month. Every dollar raised moves that research forward.
