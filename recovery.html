Where I am now

The sting isn’t there anymore

I am still having breakthrough seizures about once a month or so — that part didn’t vanish. But the fear that used to run every day before it arrived finally eased, a 24/7 DBS setting held well enough to put a weight vest and running shoes back on, and three years of quiet discipline took 154 pounds off a frame epilepsy had quietly taught to stay still.
