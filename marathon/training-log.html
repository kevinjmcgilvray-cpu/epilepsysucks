March 7, 2027 · ASICS Los Angeles Marathon

Stadium to the Stars

From Dodger Stadium to Avenue of the Stars in Century City — a USATF-certified Boston Qualifier, point-to-point through Chinatown, Hollywood, Beverly Hills, and Brentwood. On paper it’s a net downhill. In practice, it’s a rolling-hill race that punishes anyone who believes the brochure.

How the course actually runs

Miles 1–3 dump you out of Dodger Stadium on a steep downhill — the trap that burns people who go out too hot. Miles 4–6 answer with short, sharp rollers through Echo Park and Silver Lake. Miles 10–18 roll through Hollywood and West Hollywood: mostly flat with a short climb around mile 12, then a cruise toward Beverly Hills.

The fight is late. Miles 20–24 — the San Vicente out-and-back — drop hard (nearly −5% in places), then climb back. That’s the quad-buster where most PR attempts are won or lost. Hold something for Brentwood, or Century City will feel like a different race.
