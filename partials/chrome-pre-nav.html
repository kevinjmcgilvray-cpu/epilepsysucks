A note on sensory effects

This site has a Storm effects setting — brief lightning flashes with rotating thunder sounds, cycling 10 seconds on / 10 seconds off. It's off by default; turning it on from the storm icon near the top of the page shows a confirmation first. This site also automatically disables it entirely if your device's "reduce motion" accessibility setting is on. If you have photosensitive epilepsy, we'd recommend keeping it off.
