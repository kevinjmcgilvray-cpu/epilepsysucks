Before you go in — a quick flash warning

This site has a Storm effects setting — brief lightning flashes with rotating thunder sounds, cycling 10 seconds on / 10 seconds off. It's on by default, but can be switched off anytime from the storm icon near the top of the page. This site also automatically disables it entirely if your device's "reduce motion" accessibility setting is on. If you have photosensitive epilepsy, we'd recommend turning it off.
